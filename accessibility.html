Accessibility

This site aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA. WCAG 2.1 AA is the standard the U.S. Department of Justice adopted in 2024 for state and local government websites under Title II of the ADA, and the usual benchmark for ADA accessibility on other sites.

What this site does

  • A "Skip to main content" link on every page, and a link to skip past the embedded map.
  • Visible focus outlines on every link and control, with everything reachable by keyboard.
  • Text and chart colors that meet WCAG contrast ratios in both light and dark themes.
  • Every chart has a table with the same numbers. Chart values can also be read with the arrow keys, and each day's values are exposed to screen readers as the control's value text.
  • Link text that names the project it belongs to, for screen reader users.
  • Pages that reflow at 320 pixels wide and at 400% zoom without sideways scrolling.

Display settings

Use the Display settings button at the top of any page to choose:

  • Theme: match your system, light, or dark.
  • Chart colors: a colorblind-safe palette, based on the Okabe-Ito colors, that also gives each chart line its own dash pattern.
  • Contrast: higher-contrast text and borders.
  • Text size: larger text.
  • Links: always underlined.

Your choices are saved in this browser only. Without JavaScript, the site follows your system's light or dark setting.

How it was tested

On November 7, 2026, every page was checked with axe-core 4.14 (WCAG 2.0, 2.1 and 2.2 A and AA rules, plus best practices) in light and dark themes, at 1280 and 320 pixels wide, with no violations. Keyboard access, focus order and contrast ratios were also checked by hand.

Known limitations

  • The wildfire map embeds an ArcGIS Online app made by Esri. Its accessibility is outside this site's control. The layer descriptions under the map and the project's own map give the same information.
  • The K-12 attendance dashboard is a separate site with its own checks, described in its repository.
  • The site has not yet been tested by hand with a screen reader. That is planned.
